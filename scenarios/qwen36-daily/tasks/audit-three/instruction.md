Three rules, and the ordinary cases still work.

No request a member can make may leave anyone with a role they did not already have. Administrators still change roles. People still change their own name and email.

Only an editor of a book, or an administrator, may delete that book. Editors still can.

A search term is data. A title that contains a percent sign stays findable by that title, and a percent sign or an underscore alone does not match every title. Stripping those characters out of the term is a failure. Results only come from books the searcher may read.
